Privacy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Who is responsible?

Maelstrom Bergen is responsible for the personal data collected through this website. Questions and requests can be sent to contact@maelstrombergen.com.

What we collect and why

Reservations: name, email, phone number, booking details and optional notes, used to manage your request, send confirmations, reminders and cancellation messages.

Contact messages: your contact details, subject and message, used only to answer your enquiry.

Newsletter: your email address, used to send Maelstrom news and offers when you have chosen to subscribe. You can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter.

Guest gallery: the photo you submit is reviewed before publication. By submitting it, you confirm that you are allowed to share it and that identifiable people have agreed to its publication.

Storage and service providers

We keep information only as long as necessary: booking and contact records for up to 12 months, newsletter details until you unsubscribe, and approved photos until they are removed or consent is withdrawn.

We use Google services for forms and booking records, Brevo for email, and Cloudinary for guest photos. These providers process data on our behalf.

Information stored on your device

The site uses local browser storage for your language choice, newsletter display preferences, voting status and the one-photo-per-day limit. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, withdraw consent, or request removal of a photo. Contact us at contact@maelstrombergen.com. You may also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
